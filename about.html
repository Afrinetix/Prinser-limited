About Prinser Limited

Machakos Town's most trusted technology partner - powering individuals, businesses, and institutions with reliable tech since 2016.

Our Story

Born in Machakos, Built for Kenya

Prinser Limited was founded with a clear mission: to make quality technology accessible and affordable to every person in Machakos County and beyond. Starting as a small computer shop in the heart of Machakos Town, we have grown into a full-service technology company trusted by individuals, businesses, schools, and government institutions.

We understand the unique needs of the Kenyan market - from students seeking budget-friendly laptops to businesses that require robust CCTV security systems. Our team combines deep product knowledge with genuine commitment to customer satisfaction.

Today, Prinser Limited is synonymous with reliability, value, and expert local support. When technology fails you, we're just a call away - right here in Machakos.
